# Screenshots

The default content with 12 months of synthetic sample data (`make demo`), in dark mode. Every name, owner and number is generated; nothing comes from a real organisation.

## Overview

The landing page: status counts per audience dashboard, and the indicators that are red.

![Overview with status counts for the management, risk committee and team operations dashboards, and one red indicator under needs attention](assets/screenshots/01-overview.png)

## Team operations

Daily indicators with owner and action, exceptions by age, past-due findings by severity, certificates expiring by business unit, and audit requests answered on time.

![Team operations dashboard with an indicator table and four charts](assets/screenshots/02-team-operations.png)

## Management

Program indicators as trends against baseline and target.

![Management dashboard with four key control indicator tiles, trend charts and a program indicator table](assets/screenshots/03-management.png)

## Risk committee

Key risk indicators with status, change, owner and action, a trend, and status by business unit.

![Risk committee dashboard with a key risk indicator table, a trend chart for exceptions open more than 180 days, and a status grid by business unit](assets/screenshots/04-risk-committee.png)

## Metrics

Every agreed indicator with its latest value and status. Each row opens the metric detail with its definition, history and source batches.

![Metrics list with type, status, value, owner and frequency](assets/screenshots/05-metrics.png)

## Controls

Control status is the worst status of the control's metrics; controls without a metric show as unknown, never green.

![Controls list with status, owner, metrics, open exceptions and open findings](assets/screenshots/06-controls.png)

## Exceptions

The exceptions register read from the GRC tool, with aging, root causes and an expiry calendar.

![Exceptions page with summary counts, root cause bars, expiry calendar and the register](assets/screenshots/07-exceptions.png)

## Findings

Findings from audits and assessments, with days past due and repeat flags.

![Findings register sorted by days past due](assets/screenshots/08-findings.png)

## Reports

Build a signed evidence package for any of the six report types. The page shows the command and key fingerprint anyone can use to verify a package.

![Reports page with the package form and verification instructions](assets/screenshots/09-reports.png)

## Audit room

What auditors can see: their grants, the packages inside them, and every download.

![Audit room with auditor grants, packages, verification instructions and access log](assets/screenshots/10-audit-room.png)

## Audit log

The hash-chained, append-only audit log, with a button that verifies the whole chain.

![Audit log entries with sequence number, time, actor, action, target and hash](assets/screenshots/11-audit-log.png)
